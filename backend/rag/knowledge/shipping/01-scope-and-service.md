# Scope, regions and service

Policy ID: SHIP-01
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

Shipping destinations: Germany, Austria, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg and France only. No shipping to other countries, PO boxes, military addresses, parcel forwarding companies, or sanctioned destinations. Orders are accepted only after payment confirmation. Available methods: standard and express for mainland Germany; standard only for the other listed countries. Orders containing oversized, hazardous, age-restricted or temperature-sensitive goods require manual review and are not eligible for guaranteed dispatch. Business days are Monday through Friday excluding public holidays at the shipping origin (Berlin). All delivery estimates begin after dispatch, not checkout. No guaranteed arrival date unless separately confirmed in writing.

## Order acceptance and service eligibility

The delivery address entered at checkout determines availability. The billing address does not determine the destination. The customer must provide a reachable recipient, full street name, house number, postal code, city, country and an email address for shipping updates. Orders cannot be shipped to an unsupported country by entering a different billing country. If a delivery location is unsupported, checkout must reject the shipping option rather than promise later manual delivery. An order-confirmation email acknowledges the order; it is not evidence that the carrier has accepted a parcel. Shoply reserves the right to contact the customer about address validation or stock issues before dispatch. If an order cannot be fulfilled, support must explain the verified reason and offer cancellation or an alternative only where operationally possible.

## Business-day calculation examples

For a payment confirmed Monday at 13:00 Berlin time, dispatch is normally targeted by Tuesday evening; the transit estimate starts at carrier handover. A payment confirmed Friday at 16:00 is after the cutoff, so dispatch may occur on Tuesday, assuming no Berlin public holiday. Orders placed on a Sunday are treated as received for dispatch planning on the next business day. These examples are illustrative rather than guaranteed. A carrier delivery scan is not the same event as warehouse dispatch. If the warehouse has not dispatched, tracking may not exist yet. The customer should not be given a fabricated tracking number.

## Availability and unusual products

Ordinary small parcels are covered by the published table. Bulky furniture, batteries requiring special carriage, perishables, age-restricted items and products needing special packaging may have separate restrictions. Such items are not automatically eligible for standard or express shipping. The checkout must disclose any applicable limitation or charge before payment. If the catalog does not indicate whether a product is restricted, customer support must refer the case for manual review rather than infer an exemption. The policy applies to orders fulfilled by Shoply; marketplace or third-party fulfillment requires separately disclosed terms.
