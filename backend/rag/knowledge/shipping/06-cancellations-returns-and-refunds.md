# Cancellations, returns, refunds and consumer rights

Policy ID: SHIP-06
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

Before dispatch, cancellation requests can usually be fulfilled; after dispatch, cancellation may require receiving the parcel and using the returns process. For eligible EU consumer distance purchases, statutory withdrawal rights generally allow 14 days from receipt, subject to legal exceptions; this policy does not replace statutory rights. Shoply additionally offers a voluntary 30-calendar-day return window after delivery for unused items with accessories, subject to stated exceptions and legal rights. Opening ordinary product packaging alone does not automatically remove statutory withdrawal rights; handling beyond what is necessary to inspect may affect compensation under applicable law. Do not promise return eligibility for sealed hygiene goods unsealed after delivery, customized goods, or other legally exempt categories without case review. For voluntary returns, customer pays return postage unless Shoply supplied the wrong or defective item; statutory withdrawal return-cost rules and mandatory law prevail. Approved refunds go to the original payment method, typically processed within 5 business days after the legal refund prerequisites are met; bank posting may take additional time. Standard outbound shipping reimbursement on statutory withdrawal follows applicable law; premium express surcharges are not automatically refundable. Never issue or promise a refund without an authorized backend action.

## Withdrawal and voluntary return distinction

The statutory EU consumer withdrawal framework and Shoply's voluntary return offer are separate. Eligible distance-purchase consumers generally have 14 days from receipt to declare withdrawal, subject to exceptions and applicable law. Shoply's voluntary 30-day offer is an additional benefit for eligible unused goods with accessories; it does not narrow statutory rights. A customer who opened ordinary headphones after 20 days may fall outside the typical 14-day withdrawal period but could qualify for the voluntary offer if the condition rules are met. The assistant must ask about delivery date, item condition and product category rather than promise approval.

## Packaging, use and exceptions

Opening a standard product box alone does not necessarily defeat statutory withdrawal rights. Handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of goods can affect liability for diminished value under applicable law. The voluntary offer requires unused condition and included accessories. Personalized or custom-made items, certain perishable goods and sealed hygiene goods unsealed after delivery may be excluded from withdrawal under applicable legal conditions. Electronics are not automatically excluded just because packaging is opened. When a category or exception is unclear, escalate instead of denying the return.

## Return shipping and payment

For a voluntary change-of-mind return, Shoply's policy assigns return postage to the customer unless a separate offer applies. For wrong, defective or damaged goods, Shoply arranges a compliant remedy without automatically charging the customer for its error. Statutory withdrawal return costs depend on legally required disclosures and applicable law. Refunds must use the original payment method unless an authorized exception is agreed. The typical five-business-day internal processing target does not guarantee bank posting time and must not override statutory refund deadlines. Never ask for full card credentials.

## Cancellation and fulfillment status

An order in processing may still be cancellable, but the assistant cannot promise cancellation without checking the fulfillment system. Once the carrier has the parcel, cancellation may require a delivery refusal or return workflow. A customer who wants to stop an order should be offered the available verified options and, if necessary, human support. Refunds and reshipments must never be reported as completed without a corresponding authorized action.
