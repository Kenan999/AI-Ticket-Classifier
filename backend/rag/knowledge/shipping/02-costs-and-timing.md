# Shipping prices, thresholds and delivery estimates

Policy ID: SHIP-02
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

Mainland Germany: standard EUR 4.90; free standard shipping for merchandise subtotal at least EUR 60 after discounts and before shipping; express EUR 12.90 regardless of subtotal. Austria, Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg: standard EUR 9.90; France: standard EUR 12.90. No free international shipping. Typical transit after dispatch: Germany standard 2–4 business days, express 1–2 business days; Austria/Netherlands/Belgium/Luxembourg 3–6 business days; France 4–7 business days. These are estimates, not promises. Orders paid by 14:00 Berlin time on business days usually dispatch within 1 business day; after cutoff or on non-business days, within 2 business days. Stock shortages, payment verification, fraud review and exceptional demand can extend dispatch. Shipping fees are shown at checkout before payment. No Saturday or Sunday delivery promise. Remote islands or hard-to-reach areas may have longer transit and may require an additional fee disclosed before payment.

## Price calculation and mixed baskets

The EUR 60 free-standard-shipping threshold applies only to mainland Germany and to the eligible merchandise subtotal after coupons, before shipping. Gift cards, service charges and shipping fees do not count toward the threshold. If the qualifying subtotal is EUR 59.99, standard shipping is EUR 4.90; at EUR 60.00, it is free. Express shipping is EUR 12.90 even when the basket qualifies for free standard shipping. One order shipped in multiple parcels does not generate multiple outbound shipping charges. If items are cancelled before dispatch and the remaining order falls below the threshold, support must not retroactively charge shipping without the customer's informed agreement. All example amounts are Shoply policy prices in euros.

## Delivery estimate scenarios

An in-stock German standard order handed to the carrier on Tuesday is normally expected within two to four business days after Tuesday. This excludes Saturday, Sunday and relevant public holidays. Express transit of one to two business days does not mean same-day dispatch or next-day delivery from the time of purchase. International estimates exclude unexpected customs, local holidays, rural routing and carrier disruption. A delivery estimate is not a contractual guaranteed delivery date. If a customer needs an exact arrival day for a wedding or trip, support should state that the standard estimates cannot guarantee that date and offer human support if a confirmed alternative exists.

## Checkout, surcharges and refunds

Shipping fees and any known remote-area surcharge must be visible before final order submission. Support cannot invent a shipping discount after purchase. A failed express delivery target does not automatically trigger a refund of the express surcharge unless a specific binding guarantee or applicable law provides one. For a valid statutory withdrawal, refund treatment of outbound standard shipping follows mandatory consumer law; premium upgrades above the least expensive standard delivery may be excluded. Any case involving a disputed price, duplicated charge or conflicting checkout total must be escalated for verification.
