# Damage, incomplete and incorrect deliveries

Policy ID: SHIP-05
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

Customers should photograph the outer packaging, shipping label and affected item, retain packaging where possible, and contact support promptly with order number. Photos help investigation but are not an absolute prerequisite for statutory rights. A damaged or wrong item is handled without additional shipping charges to the customer. For missing items, first verify whether the order has multiple tracking numbers or partial shipments. Support investigates inventory and carrier scans. Do not promise an immediate refund, specific replacement date, or claim that damage was customer-caused without verification. Where applicable, statutory consumer remedies take precedence over these procedures.

## Damage evidence and safety

For visibly damaged outer packaging, customers may photograph the parcel before opening, but should not handle a dangerous or leaking product. If the contents are hazardous, stop use and contact support for safe instructions. Retain the packaging and shipping label if practical. Photographs and an order number help locate the shipment, but lack of photographs does not automatically extinguish statutory rights. Do not ask the customer to dispose of a damaged item until support gives verified instructions.

## Missing contents and partial deliveries

Check whether each ordered product was dispatched, whether there are multiple tracking references, and whether the carrier reports multiple parcels. A product that has not yet shipped is not the same as a product missing from a delivered parcel. If all packages show delivered and an item is absent, open an inventory and shipping investigation. Support may request a description of the box condition and packing slip, but must not demand unnecessary personal data. A replacement or refund requires an authorized decision.

## Wrong product or quantity

If the customer receives the wrong product, color, size or quantity, verify the original order and fulfillment record. The customer should not be charged extra return shipping for a Shoply fulfillment mistake. If the wrong item is expensive, regulated or dangerous, refer to human support for safe handling and return arrangements. Do not instruct customers to keep or return a product without checking the approved resolution. The assistant must never invent a return label or claim that a replacement has shipped.

## Defective versus transit-damaged

A manufacturing defect and carrier damage may involve different investigations, but customer-facing statutory remedies must still be respected. A customer does not have to identify the responsible internal department before requesting assistance. Escalate warranty, guarantee and legal-rights disputes for human review. Avoid promising that every cosmetic mark qualifies for an immediate refund without assessment.
