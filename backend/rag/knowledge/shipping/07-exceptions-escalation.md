# Exceptions, privacy and escalation

Policy ID: SHIP-07
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

Severe weather, strikes, customs checks, carrier outages, holidays, force majeure, verification holds and system errors can affect delivery. Shoply should communicate verified changes and offer support without inventing dates. Customs and import taxes are not expected for normal intra-EU deliveries in the listed destinations; unusual product-specific charges require manual review. For disputed delivery, theft allegations, vulnerable customers, legal demands, data protection issues, chargebacks or contradictory tracking, transfer to a human agent. Ask only for order number and information needed to investigate; never request full card details, passwords or identity documents in chat. Policy information is general and cannot override mandatory consumer law. If the documents do not answer a question, explicitly say so and offer human support. Do not invent a carrier, tracking number, delivery event, refund approval or legal guarantee.

## Severe weather and carrier disruptions

Weather, floods, transport strikes, road closures, carrier IT outages and holiday backlogs may delay dispatch or transit. Explain the verified disruption and its likely scope without inventing a reopening date or a guaranteed arrival. Customers should receive updates through official order channels where available. Express shipping does not bypass a closed transport network. A disruption does not automatically remove statutory consumer rights or excuse misleading delivery claims.

## Fraud prevention and privacy

For suspected payment fraud, address mismatch or unauthorized purchases, the order may be held for verification. Support must never request passwords, one-time codes, complete payment card numbers or copies of identity documents in the ordinary chat. Verification should use an approved secure process. Share order-specific information only after customer authorization checks. If a customer asks to delete personal data, refer the request to the privacy workflow rather than promising immediate deletion of legally retained transaction records.

## Escalation and evidence hierarchy

Use verified order-management data for a specific order's state; use shipping policy documents for general rules. If a policy conflicts with a legal obligation, mandatory law prevails. If a model answer conflicts with a source document, the source document prevails. If two documents conflict, do not silently choose; report the ambiguity and escalate. The assistant must state when no reliable source covers a scenario. For complaints involving loss, theft, repeated delivery failures, accessibility needs, disputes, chargebacks or threatened legal action, offer a human agent and preserve relevant non-sensitive context.

## Customer communication examples

A suitable answer to a tracking delay is: “Your tracking may take up to 24 hours to show its first carrier scan after handover. I cannot verify your parcel's current location without its order record.” A suitable answer to an unsupported country is: “Shoply's shipping policy currently lists only Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg and France.” A suitable answer to an uncertain refund is: “I can explain the policy, but eligibility and approval depend on your order and the applicable consumer rights.” These examples are guidance, not proof of a real order action.
