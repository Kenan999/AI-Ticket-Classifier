# Delays, missing parcels and failed delivery

Policy ID: SHIP-04
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

If delivery exceeds the estimated window, customers should check tracking and contact support with order number. Shoply investigates with the carrier; do not label a parcel lost solely from an estimate being exceeded. For domestic shipments with no tracking movement for 5 business days after the last scan, support may open a carrier investigation; for international shipments after 8 business days. Investigation is not an automatic refund or replacement. If carrier confirms loss, Shoply offers replacement if stock is available or a refund to the original payment method. If carrier marks delivered but customer cannot locate the parcel, first check safe place, household members and neighbors, then contact support within 7 calendar days for investigation; do not automatically deny claims after that period. For unsuccessful delivery, the carrier may attempt redelivery or hold the parcel at a pickup point; holding periods depend on carrier and must be checked in tracking. If returned as undeliverable, Shoply contacts customer to arrange reshipment or cancellation. Additional shipping costs caused by an incorrect address may be charged only after customer approval and subject to applicable law.

## Investigation sequence

First verify the order number, dispatch date, destination, last verified carrier scan and whether the promised window was an estimate. Next check for service alerts, delivery attempts, pickup-point notices and split shipments. For Germany, a carrier investigation may be opened after five business days without scan movement; for international parcels, after eight business days. These are investigation thresholds, not automatic loss determinations. Record the investigation reference if available and explain that a further update depends on the carrier. Do not tell customers that the parcel is lost before verification.

## Delivered but not received

If tracking shows delivered, ask the customer to check the mailbox, safe place, household members, building reception and neighbors where appropriate. Request contact within seven calendar days to facilitate investigation, but do not present that period as forfeiture of mandatory rights. Support must review proof of delivery and delivery instructions, and escalate conflicting evidence. Do not assume the customer is dishonest. A delivery photo or signature is evidence to evaluate, not an automatic resolution. A replacement or refund requires a confirmed decision or applicable legal entitlement.

## Failed attempts and returned parcels

A courier may attempt delivery more than once or leave a pickup notice, depending on the carrier. Pickup holding periods vary; the assistant must not invent a fixed number of days. The customer should consult verified tracking for the actual pickup deadline. If a parcel returns to Shoply, support checks the reason and whether the goods are received and saleable. A new shipment requires confirmed address details, stock and any agreed charge. If the failure was caused by Shoply or its carrier, the customer should not automatically bear extra costs. If the customer refuses delivery, refund and withdrawal rights must be evaluated under applicable law.

## Long delay and remedies

When an order is substantially delayed, the customer may have statutory rights that differ from Shoply's estimated windows. The assistant should not say that an investigation suspends all consumer rights. A request for cancellation, replacement or refund must be assessed against the order status, contractual terms and mandatory law. Where facts are disputed, transfer to human support and preserve the case history. Do not promise a specific resolution deadline unless the operational system confirms it.
