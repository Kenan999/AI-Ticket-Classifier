# Tracking, split shipments and address changes

Policy ID: SHIP-03
Effective: 2026-11-23
Document owner: Shoply Customer Service

A tracking link is issued by email after carrier handover; first scan can take up to 24 hours. Tracking updates can be delayed or missing; absence of a scan alone does not prove loss. Multiple items may ship in separate parcels at no extra shipping fee, with separate tracking links. A customer may request address correction before dispatch; Shoply cannot guarantee a change after carrier handover. If the address is incomplete or incorrect, the customer must contact support immediately. Changes of delivery country or method after checkout require cancellation and a new order if still possible. Never claim to have changed an address or intercepted a parcel unless a confirmed operational tool reports success. Customer-support chat cannot independently modify a live order.

## Tracking lifecycle

Typical tracking states are label created, carrier accepted, in transit, out for delivery, delivery attempted, available for pickup, delivered and returned to sender. Label created does not prove physical carrier handover. The first scan may appear within 24 hours after handover, and subsequent updates may be delayed. Tracking is informational; an outdated status is not itself proof of theft, loss or delivery. The assistant must not translate a generic in-transit scan into a precise delivery appointment. If the tracking URL is unavailable in verified order data, explain that it cannot be provided yet and suggest checking the dispatch email or contacting support.

## Address correction workflow

Before dispatch, collect the order number and the corrected destination details through an approved support channel. Check whether warehouse processing still permits edits. Never state that an edit succeeded before an authorized order-management action confirms it. After carrier handover, a carrier redirect may be possible but is never guaranteed; availability depends on the service and destination. A change of recipient, destination country or shipping method may be refused for security or carrier reasons. Do not ask customers to post full personal addresses publicly. For an address that was incorrect at checkout, the case must be investigated before determining responsibility for additional costs.

## Split shipments and duplicate notifications

One order may contain several packages, each with its own tracking reference and arrival date. A partial delivery is not automatically a missing-item incident until all parcel references have been checked. The order page should distinguish shipped, pending and delivered line items. Duplicate tracking emails do not necessarily mean a duplicate purchase. If a customer reports duplicate deliveries, the support agent must verify order and parcel records rather than ask the customer to pay for an unsolicited item.
